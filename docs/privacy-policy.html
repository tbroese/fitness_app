Privacy Policy for Inertia

Effective Date: November 17, 2026 • Developer: Tanner Broese • Contact: tannerbroese@gmail.com

Inertia ("we", "our", or "us") is a gym consistency and workout streak tracking mobile application (package ID: com.tannerbroese.fitnesstracker). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how you can delete your data.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Profile Information: When you sign in with Google (via Supabase Auth), we collect your name and email address to identify your account and sync your progress across devices. You may optionally provide your age, gender, and personal record (PR) lift weights for leaderboard filtering and profile display.
  • Location Data (Foreground & Background): With your explicit permission, Inertia accesses your device's location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, and ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION) to calculate distance to your selected gym and automatically verify when you stay inside your gym's perimeter for at least 15 minutes — even when the app is closed or in the background.
  • Workout & Social Activity: Verified gym check-in timestamps, workout durations, selected gym names, weekly goals, streak counts, and mutual friend connections.

Location Privacy Guarantee: Your continuous GPS coordinates are processed on-device to detect gym arrival and dwell time. We do not store your continuous GPS location history on our servers, and we never sell or share your location data with advertisers or data brokers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Verify 15-minute gym visits and update your Personal and Verified Gym streaks.
  • Sync your profile, workout history, and streaks to our cloud database (Supabase).
  • Display your rank and stats on the global, gym, and friends leaderboards.
  • Support optional friend requests and mutual friend leaderboards.

3. Third-Party Services

Inertia uses trusted third-party infrastructure providers strictly to operate the app:

  • Google Sign-In: Used for optional account authentication.
  • Supabase: Used for authentication and cloud storage of your profile, verified workouts, and friend connections.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your account and workout data for as long as your account remains active. You can permanently delete your account and all associated cloud and local data at any time directly inside the app:

  • Open Inertia → go to the Profile tab → tap the Settings gear icon → tap Delete Account.

Deleting your account immediately removes your profile, workout logs, and friend requests from our cloud database and clears local app data on your device. You may also request manual deletion by emailing tannerbroese@gmail.com.

5. Children's Privacy

Inertia is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at tannerbroese@gmail.com.
